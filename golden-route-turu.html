Golden Route — Under the Winter Sun

From Osaka to Tokyo — four ancient cities and Mt. Fuji on one route

Japan's Golden Route — the country's four most-loved cities on a single itinerary. Entering through Kansai and departing from Narita, you see Osaka, Nara, Kyoto, the Fuji area and Tokyo without ever retracing your steps.

Winter is Japan's clearest season: Mt. Fuji is visible far more often at this time of year. Departing from Istanbul with a Turkish-speaking guide and a direct Turkish Airlines flight — airfare included in the price.

Day by day

The 8-day programme

018 Dec · Tue

Istanbul → Osaka

Departure from Istanbul on an overnight Turkish Airlines flight, airport transfer and hotel check-in.

029 Dec · Wed

Osaka

Osaka Castle and park, the historic "Osaka's Kitchen" market with a welcome dinner, and an evening stroll through Namba, Hozenji Yokocho and Dotonbori.

0310 Dec · Thu

Nara · Osaka

Nara Park's free-roaming deer, the Great Buddha at Todai-ji, the Naramachi quarter, then shopping back in Osaka at Shinsaibashi and Namba Parks.

0411 Dec · Fri

Kyoto

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove, the red gates of Fushimi Inari, Kiyomizu-dera with the Ninenzaka–Sannenzaka lanes, and the Gion geisha district.

0512 Dec · Sat

Osaka → Tokyo

Bullet train to Tokyo, a tea ceremony in Asakusa, Kaminarimon, Nakamise and Senso-ji Temple, then Shibuya Crossing and the Hachiko statue.

0613 Dec · Sun

Mt. Fuji · Kawaguchiko

The Fuji reflection on Lake Kawaguchiko, the spring village of Oshino Hakkai and Saiko Iyashi-no-Sato, then back to Tokyo.

0714 Dec · Mon

Tokyo

Meiji Jingu Shrine, Shinjuku's grand department stores, the luxury avenues of Ginza, and tax-free shopping at Don Quijote.

0815 Dec · Tue

Tokyo → Istanbul

After breakfast, private transfer to the airport and return flight to Istanbul.
